About Pegwatch

Same stock. A clearer view.

Pegwatch compares tokenized stocks across blockchains and issuers, so you can see which version of a stock tracks the real share best and where it trades.

What we stand for

Three principles

01

Independent

No issuer or exchange pays for a place in the table. The order follows the column you sort by, nothing else.

02

Transparent

Every price links back to the public market it came from, and the method is written out on the front page.

03

Non-custodial

Pegwatch is read-only. No wallet connection, no deposits, no sign-up.

Data

Where the numbers come from

Token lists and share ratios come from each issuer’s public data. On-chain prices, market depth and volume come from DexScreener, which indexes the exchanges on each chain. Share prices come from live quotes for the underlying stock.

Business model

How Pegwatch stays free

Pegwatch is free and has no paid placements. If we add sponsored spots later, they’ll be clearly labelled and kept outside the table.

Disclaimer
